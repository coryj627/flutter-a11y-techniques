Source read, not measured: Flutter's web engine takes the operating system
from navigator.platform ("Mac..." macOS, or iOS with more than two touch
points; "iPhone", "iPad", "iPod" iOS; an Android user agent Android;
"Linux..." Linux; "Win..." Windows; detectOperatingSystem in
ui_web/browser_detection.dart), and defaultTargetPlatform on the web
follows it (foundation/_platform_web.dart). ExpansionTile's hint is
"Collapsed\n double tap to expand" on iOS and macOS and "Collapsed"
elsewhere, with a live region on Android (material/expansion_tile.dart,
_buildHeader). CupertinoExpansionTile's hint is the same on iOS and macOS
and none elsewhere (cupertino/expansion_tile.dart, _buildHeader).

Not run: a real browser on Windows; a browser on Android; on Linux,
anything after this change (CI will).
