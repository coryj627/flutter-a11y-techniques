VoiceOver's last phrase and the position of its cursor are read over AppleScript after every move: a move that changes the position, or that speaks a different phrase, is a stop; two moves in a row that do neither are the end. A wrong stop, or a walk that reached the end with any expected stop not read, fails the test for good; only a walk cut short before the end with every stop right so far, or one with a stop read as an empty phrase (a failed capture, which never passes) and nothing wrong read, is walked again, up to three walks, each shown as its own row (`-walk-<n>`). VoiceOver's own help message ("You are currently on ...") is left out only at the cursor position of the stop before it, when that stop's spoken role is the help's. No runner retries
