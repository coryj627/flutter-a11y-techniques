- Method: focus the Back button, then `voiceOver.next()` to the end of the screen. VoiceOver's last phrase and the position of its cursor are read over AppleScript after every move: a move that changes the position, or that speaks a different phrase, is a stop; two moves in a row that do neither are the end. Every walk must read the whole screen, each stop in its place (web_tests/vo/c18_vo_walks.js): the title and the description, the "Example" heading, and the expected list after it. A wrong stop, or a walk that reached the end with any expected stop not read, fails the test for good; only a walk cut short before the end with every stop right so far is walked again, up to three walks, each shown as its own row (`-walk-<n>`). No runner retries
