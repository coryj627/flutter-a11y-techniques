Generated by `python3 tool/voiceover_web.py V01`. Do not edit by hand.

- Date: 2026-11-28

- Browsers: Playwright 1.65.0 Chromium and WebKit builds (WebKit is not Safari itself)
- Guidepup: 0.40.0
- Flutter: 3.47.5
- Method: focus the Back button and capture VoiceOver's last phrase and its cursor's position there (the walk's start row), then `voiceOver.next()` to the end of the screen, capturing both over AppleScript after every move (collectWalk, web_tests/vo/v01_vo_walks.js, S16's walk with the S08 to S12 judge update). Every phrase is recorded with its white space, a line break included, made single spaces (the Cupertino fields' names hold one). A move that changes the position, or that speaks a non-empty phrase other than the stop before it (or than the start, before the first stop), is a stop, and the position at each stop is the walk's positions row. A move that does neither is kept: one followed by a move on is an unresolved read, a gap after the stop before it, and is listed on the walk's unresolved row; two in a row are the end only when a move back then reads the stop before the last, not empty, at another position, and a move forward the last again at its position, both on the walk's end row; the move back may instead read VoiceOver's help about the stop before the last, naming its spoken role, at exactly that stop's recorded position; a failed witness is wrong for good. Every walk is judged whole (judgeWalk): the title, the description, a bad example's defect, the "Example" heading, and the expected list after it, each in its place; a stop past the last expected one, empty or not, is wrong; a wrong stop always wins, across a gap the next stop must be the next expected one or the one after it, and a walk that ran out of moves is wrong. Only a walk with no wrong stop and a gap, or a stop read as an empty phrase, is walked again, up to three walks, each its own row (`-walk-<n>`, a "/" in a stop written "\/"), and it never passes. VoiceOver's own help message ("You are currently on ..." or "in ...") is left out only at the position of the stop before it, whose reading speaks the role the help names; it stays in the walk's row, and the web tree spec judges the transcript again from its rows by the same rule, from the positions row. Relaxed for the text fields only, by the principal's decisions of 2026-11-28 after the first recording (docs/evidence/probes/v01/voiceover-web-round1-fail.md.txt), because a field with a hint gets aria-label "Email\nRequired" and placeholder "Required" when VoiceOver's cursor gives it focus: the three fields with a hint (good-material's Email field, good-cupertino's and bad-cupertino-placeholder's) are each one stop judged by the pattern `^Email( Required){0,2} edit text$`, so how many times "Required" is read is recorded, not judged; such a field read again in a form of that pattern at exactly its position is that stop read again, not a second stop: it stays in the walk's row and its position is written "re-read <bounds>" on the positions row, and the transcript judge takes a re-read only from that mark; when the last stop is one of those three fields or bad-faint-edge's field (no hint, its words judged exactly), the end witness's move forward may read an empty phrase or another form of that stop, at exactly its recorded position (for bad-faint-edge only "Email edit text" or empty); the move back must still freshly read the stop before the last, not empty, at another position, and may read it in its other field form only when that stop is a field with a hint, at exactly its recorded position (good-cupertino). Every other stop is judged exactly, with no such leniency. No runner retries. The browser's colour scheme is Playwright's default, light. VoiceOver's last phrase is text: a colour is not in it, so what is judged is the words and the stops, not the contrast
