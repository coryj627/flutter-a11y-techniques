- Method: focus the Back button, wait until VoiceOver has been quiet for two seconds, then `voiceOver.next()` to the end of the screen. VoiceOver's last phrase and the position of its cursor are read over AppleScript after every move: a move that changes the position, or that speaks a different phrase, is a stop; two moves in a row that do neither are the end. Each example is walked as it opens, and again ("-changed") after its first control has been clicked once; the change count, the last element of every example, must then read "Changes: 1". No test is retried by Playwright; a walk that fell short is walked again by the test itself, up to three times, and a walk with a wrong reading fails the test with no further walk. Every stop of a walk is judged, the screen's own stops before the heading included. Every try is a row of its own, `<slug>-walk-<n>`, with the verdict and the stops after the "Example" heading for a complete walk, every stop for any other. With no retries, a test the tool names under "Walks that needed a retry" failed and was not retried. The `control-html-walk-<n>` rows are a plain HTML page with no Flutter, each walk shown whole
