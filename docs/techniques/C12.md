# C12 Tooltip

A tooltip shows on hover and on a long press; the pointer can move onto it;
Escape hides it; it stays until the pointer leaves or it is dismissed; and
its words reach a screen reader as the control's name or description, with
the control keeping its own name and role. Flutter's tooltip does not show
on keyboard focus.

Code: [`lib/techniques/c12_tooltip/examples.dart`](../../lib/techniques/c12_tooltip/examples.dart).
Spec: [`docs/specs/C12-tooltip.md`](../specs/C12-tooltip.md).
In the app: `/t/C12`, and each example at `/t/C12/<slug>`.

Platforms: Android, iOS, and the web apply, each with limitations of the
framework's tooltip that have no workaround in the SDK. On every platform
a tooltip never shows on keyboard focus
([FL-248](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-248)), and while it is shown its text
is a node of its own in the accessibility tree, inside the control
([FL-249](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-249)). On iOS and the web its words
join the control's name ([FL-251](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-251)).

On the web the good examples are good only with a qualification. Escape
does not hide a tooltip while no element of the page has focus
([FL-482](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-482)), and every good example's
tooltip covers other text (the line under its control, or the heading above
it where it is flipped). In that state they fail 1.4.13's dismissible
condition on the web. Escape works whenever something in the page has
focus: the Back button on a freshly loaded example, the Flutter view after
any click in it, or the control. The tests reach the failing state with
`blur()`; how a real user reaches it was not measured.

A keyboard user never sees a tooltip
([FL-248](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-248)): APG's pattern shows one on
focus, and Flutter has not added that. It fails no criterion as written,
1.4.13 included; on an icon-only button a sighted keyboard user loses the
visible text of its name.

Every example is Flutter's own `Tooltip`, configured only through its own
parameters (the owner's rule of 2026-11-21): nothing is built to fill what
it cannot do.

## Why it matters

A tooltip is the words a pointer finds by resting on a control: "Delete
draft" for a bin icon. People who magnify the screen, or use a large
pointer, need to move onto those words to read them, and need them to stay
until they are done, and to go when they press Escape, because they lie
over the page. Keyboard users never rest a pointer anywhere, and touch
users get the words only from a long press. And a screen reader user should
hear the words once, as what the control is called or what it does.

Flutter's tooltip gets several of these right and some wrong by default:
an `IconButton`'s tooltip goes as soon as the pointer moves onto it, and no
Flutter tooltip ever shows for the keyboard.

## WCAG 2.2

- [1.4.13 Content on Hover or Focus](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#content-on-hover-or-focus): content that a hover shows can be dismissed without moving the pointer (unless it covers nothing), hovered without it going, and stays until then. The exception for content the user agent draws (HTML's `title`) does not cover a Flutter tooltip, which the app draws. A long press is neither hover nor focus, so 1.4.13 does not apply to the touch case.
- [4.1.2 Name, Role, Value](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#name-role-value): the tooltip's words are a name or a description, and the control keeps its own name and role.

The failure for content shown on hover that the pointer cannot reach is
[F95](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/failures/F95). HTML's own
tooltip is the [`title` attribute](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Global_attributes/title),
which the browser draws, and which MDN advises against for touch, keyboard,
and screen reader users. The iOS reference repository has no tooltip; its
nearest is [TipKit](https://github.com/cvs-health/ios-swiftui-accessibility-techniques/blob/main/iOSswiftUIa11yTechniques/Documentation/TipKit.md),
an onboarding tip with a close button, not shown on hover or focus: its
advice that a tip's words say what they explain carries over, and the rest
is C11 Popover's. The Android reference repositories have no tooltip entry.

## Good examples

| Example | How | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `good-material` | `Tooltip(message: 'Delete draft', ignorePointer: false, excludeFromSemantics: true)` round an `IconButton` named by `Icon(semanticLabel: 'Delete draft')`. `ignorePointer: false` lets the pointer move onto the tooltip ([FL-247](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-247)). | [Understanding 1.4.13](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/content-on-hover-or-focus.html), [Flutter: Tooltip](https://api.flutter.dev/flutter/material/Tooltip-class.html) |
| `good-cupertino` | Cupertino has no tooltip: Material's `Tooltip(message:, ignorePointer: false)` round an icon-only `CupertinoButton`, which it names. | [APG: Tooltip](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/tooltip/) |
| `good-description` | `MergeSemantics` round `Tooltip(message: 'Saves a PDF copy', ignorePointer: false)` round an `OutlinedButton` "Download". TalkBack reads it after the role; on iOS and the web it joins the name ([FL-251](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-251)). | APG: Tooltip (a description) |

What makes each of them work:

- **`ignorePointer: false`**, which `IconButton.tooltip` cannot pass: its
  tooltip goes when the pointer reaches it (`bad-not-hoverable`).
- **The tooltip on the control's node.** A Material button is a semantics
  container, so a `Tooltip` round it lands outside it
  ([FL-040](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-040), `bad-outside-button`):
  name the button another way and leave the tooltip's copy out
  (`excludeFromSemantics`, as its documentation says), or merge them
  (`MergeSemantics`). A `CupertinoButton` is not a container, and the
  tooltip reaches it.
- **A focus outline that meets 3:1**, through the button's own `style` or
  `focusColor`: Material's tint on an `IconButton` and Cupertino's default
  ring are under it ([FL-430](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-430),
  [FL-431](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-431)).

What none of them can do: show on keyboard focus (FL-248), keep its words
out of the accessibility tree while shown (FL-249), or hide on Escape on a web page
with no focus (FL-482). The tests assert each, written to fail when Flutter
changes.

## Bad examples

Each has one defect, and the tests assert that the defect is present.

| Example | Defect |
| --- | --- |
| `bad-not-hoverable` | `IconButton(tooltip: 'Delete draft')` as it comes: the tooltip goes when the pointer moves onto it ([FL-247](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-247)). |
| `bad-hover-only` | A password rule only in the `Tooltip` of an information icon that takes no focus. A pointer or a long press shows it; no key does; VoiceOver on iOS passes over it ([FL-252](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-252)). |
| `bad-repeats-name` | A tooltip with the button's own text: on iOS and the web the button is named "Download Download". |
| `bad-outside-button` | `Tooltip(message:)` round an `IconButton`: the tooltip stays on a node of its own, and the button has no name ([FL-040](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-040)). |

What each fails, by platform, as the criteria are written:

| Example | Android | iOS | Web |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `bad-not-hoverable` | 1.4.13 (F95), with a mouse | none measured (no hover in XCUITest) | 1.4.13 (F95) |
| `bad-hover-only` | 2.1.1 | 2.1.1; and VoiceOver does not reach the rule | 2.1.1 |
| `bad-repeats-name` | none (TalkBack reads it once) | none (poor practice) | none (poor practice) |
| `bad-outside-button` | 4.1.2 | 4.1.2 | 4.1.2 |

No tooltip that times out is shown: under hover a Flutter tooltip stays,
and the only timeout, 1.5 s after a touch, is not a 1.4.13 matter.

### Shared code

Used by the examples below.

```dart
/// Lays out one example: the control, a line of text that the tooltip lies
/// over while it is shown, and a count of the control's activations, so
/// that people and tests can confirm the control still works.
class _Demo extends StatefulWidget {
  const _Demo({required this.slug, required this.line, required this.builder});

  final String slug;
  final String line;
  final Widget Function(BuildContext context, VoidCallback activate) builder;

  @override
  State<_Demo> createState() => _DemoState();
}

class _DemoState extends State<_Demo> {
  int _count = 0;

  @override
  Widget build(BuildContext context) {
    return Column(
      crossAxisAlignment: CrossAxisAlignment.start,
      spacing: 8,
      children: [
        widget.builder(context, () => setState(() => _count++)),
        Text(widget.line),
        Semantics(
          key: ValueKey<String>('c12-${widget.slug}-count'),
          identifier: 'c12-${widget.slug}-count',
          liveRegion: true,
          child: Text('Activations: $_count'),
        ),
      ],
    );
  }
}

/// A 2 pixel outline in the page's text colour while a Material button has
/// keyboard focus, through the button's own `style`. Material's own focus
/// tint on an `IconButton` is under 3:1 against the page
/// (docs/flutter-limitations.md, FL-430).
ButtonStyle focusRing(ColorScheme colors) => ButtonStyle(
  side: WidgetStateProperty.resolveWith(
    (states) => states.contains(WidgetState.focused)
        ? BorderSide(color: colors.onSurface, width: 2)
        : null,
  ),
);
```

### `good-material`

```dart
/// An icon button with Material's `Tooltip`. `ignorePointer: false` lets
/// the pointer move onto the tooltip; a `Tooltip` with a plain message
/// ignores the pointer by default, and `IconButton.tooltip` always does
/// (FL-247). A Material button is a semantics container, so a tooltip round
/// it does not reach it (FL-040); the button is named by its icon's label,
/// and `excludeFromSemantics: true` leaves Tooltip's own copy out, as its
/// documentation says to when the app gives the label.
class GoodMaterial extends StatelessWidget {
  const GoodMaterial({super.key});

  @override
  Widget build(BuildContext context) {
    return _Demo(
      slug: 'good-material',
      line: 'Drafts are kept for 30 days.',
      builder: (context, activate) => TestId(
        'c12-good-material-delete',
        child: Tooltip(
          message: 'Delete draft',
          ignorePointer: false,
          excludeFromSemantics: true,
          child: IconButton(
            onPressed: activate,
            style: focusRing(Theme.of(context).colorScheme),
            icon: const Icon(
              Icons.delete_outline,
              semanticLabel: 'Delete draft',
            ),
          ),
        ),
      ),
    );
  }
}
```

### `good-cupertino`

```dart
/// Cupertino has no tooltip widget, so this is Material's `Tooltip` on an
/// icon-only `CupertinoButton`. A `CupertinoButton` is not a semantics
/// container, so the tooltip reaches it and is its name, as
/// `IconButton.tooltip` names an `IconButton`.
class GoodCupertino extends StatelessWidget {
  const GoodCupertino({super.key});

  @override
  Widget build(BuildContext context) {
    return _Demo(
      slug: 'good-cupertino',
      line: 'Drafts are kept for 30 days.',
      builder: (context, activate) => TestId(
        'c12-good-cupertino-delete',
        child: Tooltip(
          message: 'Delete draft',
          ignorePointer: false,
          // Flutter 3.47.5: on the web Enter is ButtonActivateIntent, which
          // CupertinoButton does not handle (FL-002, as in C01's
          // good-cupertino); Space works.
          child: Actions(
            actions: {
              ButtonActivateIntent: CallbackAction<ButtonActivateIntent>(
                onInvoke: (_) {
                  activate();
                  return null;
                },
              ),
            },
            child: CupertinoButton(
              onPressed: activate,
              // Cupertino's default focus ring is under 3:1 on a light page
              // (FL-431).
              focusColor: CupertinoColors.label.resolveFrom(context),
              child: const Icon(CupertinoIcons.delete),
            ),
          ),
        ),
      ),
    );
  }
}
```

```dart
/// A `Tooltip` put round an `IconButton`, as one is put round any widget.
/// A Material button is a semantics container, so the tooltip's text stays
/// on a node of its own outside it, and the button has no name.
class BadOutsideButton extends StatelessWidget {
  const BadOutsideButton({super.key});

  @override

    return _Demo(
      slug: 'bad-outside-button',

      // No TestId: its MergeSemantics would put the tooltip on the button.
      // The container stands for a row or a card round the control; the
      // tooltip's node is that container's.
      builder: (context, activate) => Semantics(
        container: true,
        identifier: 'c12-bad-outside-button-tooltip',
        child: Tooltip(
          // Defect: the tooltip is outside the button's semantics.
          message: 'Delete draft',
          ignorePointer: false,
          child: IconButton(
            onPressed: activate,
            style: focusRing(Theme.of(context).colorScheme),
            icon: const Icon(Icons.delete_outline),

        ),
      ),
    );
  }
```

## What each platform exposes

Measured on Flutter 3.47.5. Screen reader speech is from the transcripts
below and the probe ([evidence/probes/c12](../evidence/probes/c12/README.md));
the rest from the tree tests and the probe.

| | Web | Android | iOS |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| An icon button's tooltip as its name (`bad-not-hoverable`, `good-cupertino`) | a button named by it; VoiceOver "Delete draft button" | a `Button` named by it, with it as its tooltip text; TalkBack "Delete draft, Button" | a button labelled by it; "Delete draft Button" |
| A tooltip merged into a button with text (`good-description`) | the name "Saves a PDF copy Download", the tooltip first; VoiceOver "Saves a PDF copy Download button" | name "Download", tooltip text "Saves a PDF copy"; TalkBack "Download, Button, Saves a PDF copy" | label "Download\nSaves a PDF copy"; "Download Saves a PDF copy Button" |
| A hover | shows it; the pointer can move onto it only with `ignorePointer: false` | the same, with a mouse | not measured |
| Keyboard focus | shows nothing | shows nothing | shows nothing |
| A long press | — | shows it, for 1.5 s after the touch | shows it, for 1.5 s after the touch |
| Escape | hides it while the page has focus; not while it has none | hid one shown by a touch (probe) | not measured |
| While it is shown | its text is an element of the page | its text is a focusable `View` | a static text element in the probe; in the examples, merged by `TestId`, the tree did not change |

Things to know:

- **`IconButton.tooltip` cannot be hovered**, and has no parameter to
  change it. Put a `Tooltip` round the button instead, and name the button
  by its icon's label.
- **Only Android treats a tooltip as a description.** iOS adds it to the
  name after the control's text, the web before it. A tooltip that repeats
  the control's text is read twice on both.
- **A tooltip alone is silent to VoiceOver on iOS.** An information icon
  explained only by its `Tooltip` is passed over; give it a label.
- **A tooltip round a Material button misses it**, as a hint does: a
  button is a semantics container.
- **No Flutter tooltip shows on keyboard focus.** A keyboard user can
  still use the control, but never sees its tooltip.

## Flutter limitations

Found on Flutter 3.47.5. Full detail is in the
[limitations log](../flutter-limitations.md).

- [FL-247](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-247): a tooltip with a plain message, and every `IconButton.tooltip`, cannot be hovered.
- [FL-248](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-248): no Flutter tooltip shows on keyboard focus.
- [FL-249](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-249): while shown, a tooltip's text is a node of its own inside its control.
- [FL-250](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-250): a `RawTooltip` is placed over its control by default.
- [FL-251](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-251): a tooltip is part of the name on iOS and the web.
- [FL-252](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-252): VoiceOver on iOS passes over a node with only a tooltip.
- [FL-253](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-253): a `FilledButton` is under 4.5:1 with focus and a hover.
- [FL-040](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-040): a `Tooltip` round a Material button stays on a node of its own.
- [FL-482](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-482): on the web, Escape does not hide a tooltip while the page has no keyboard focus.
- [FL-008](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-008): in C01, VoiceOver in Chromium read an `IconButton`'s tooltip twice; in C12's walks it read each button once.
