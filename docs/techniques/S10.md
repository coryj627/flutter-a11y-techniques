# S10 Content group replacement

A composite whose pieces do not say what it means, such as a rating drawn
as a bar or as five stars beside two numbers, is exposed as one node whose
label does, worked out from the same values the pieces show. Every piece
is hidden, a piece with semantics of its own included, and no control is
hidden with them.

Code: [`lib/techniques/s10_content_group_replacement/examples.dart`](../../lib/techniques/s10_content_group_replacement/examples.dart).
Spec: [`docs/specs/S10-content-group-replacement.md`](../specs/S10-content-group-replacement.md).
In the app: `/t/S10`, and each example at `/t/S10/<slug>`.

Platforms: Android, iOS, and the web apply. Every good example is
Flutter's own `Semantics` through its documented parameters; none is a
custom solution
([custom-solutions.md](../custom-solutions.md#s10-content-group-replacement)).
A drawn control given a framework control's role, value, and actions, the
iOS reference's `accessibilityRepresentation`, has no Flutter counterpart
([FL-025](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-025)) and is not built here; S14's
star rating and F10's slider drawn by hand do it by hand; both are
flagged custom solutions, tested in isolation and not vetted by a real-world example, and neither is recommended. That subject is
outside S10, whose requirement is a text alternative for a group, which
the good examples meet on iOS (the principal's decision).

## Why it matters

Someone who sees three and a half gold stars beside "(856)" knows at a
glance that the thing is rated 3.4 out of 5 by 856 people. A screen reader
gets only what the pieces expose: here "3.4" and "(856)", because the
stars have no text, and nothing to say what either number is. Merging the
pieces into one node does not fix it: their texts are joined, and a
progress bar drawn as the rating brings its own value, so the screen
reader says "68" as well. The fix is one node that says, in words, what
the whole means: "Rating: 3.4 out of 5 stars, based on 856 reviews", with
the pieces hidden, kept in step with what is shown, and with no button
hidden inside it.

## WCAG 2.2

- [1.3.1 Info and Relationships](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#info-and-relationships): what the composite shows, a rating out of five from a number of reviews, is available in text. With no replacement, the stars and what the numbers count are conveyed by presentation alone; with a label that does not follow the values, the shown count is not determinable.
- [4.1.2 Name, Role, Value](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#name-role-value): a control inside a replaced composite keeps its name and role.

The sources: the Android reference repositories'
[Content Group Replacement (View)](https://github.com/cvs-health/android-view-accessibility-techniques/blob/main/doc/grouping/ContentGroupReplacement.md)
and [Content Group Replacement (Compose)](https://github.com/cvs-health/android-compose-accessibility-techniques/blob/main/doc/content/ContentGroupReplacement.md)
(a rating row replaced with one description, its pieces hidden), and the
iOS reference repository's
[Accessibility Representation](https://github.com/cvs-health/ios-swiftui-accessibility-techniques/blob/main/iOSswiftUIa11yTechniques/Documentation/AccessibilityRepresentation.md)
(a drawn slider given a native `Slider`'s representation). The inventory
names no APG pattern and no HTML element.

## Good examples

| Example | How | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `good-exclude-semantics` | `Semantics(label:, excludeSemantics: true)` round the references' rating row ("Rating:", a `LinearProgressIndicator`, "3.4 / 5", "Reviews: 856"): one node, read as "Rating: 3.4 out of 5 stars, based on 856 reviews". The label is worked out from the values, so after "Add a 5-star review" it says 857. | [Flutter: Semantics.excludeSemantics](https://api.flutter.dev/flutter/widgets/Semantics/excludeSemantics.html), Android View, Android Compose |
| `good-hidden-pieces` | The same row in `MergeSemantics` round `Semantics(label:)`, each of its four pieces, the bar included, in `ExcludeSemantics`: the same one node, made the Compose reference's other way. | Android Compose, [Flutter: ExcludeSemantics](https://api.flutter.dev/flutter/widgets/ExcludeSemantics-class.html) |
| `good-stars` | Five star icons, "3.4", and "(856)" in `Semantics(label:, excludeSemantics: true)`. | Android View, Android Compose |

What makes them work:

- **`excludeSemantics: true`** drops every child's semantics: the texts,
  and the bar, which is a node of its own with the value 68 (and a
  progress bar role on Android and the web). A `Semantics(label:)`
  without it is not a label over its children: it merges them into its
  node (`bad-not-excluded`).
- **Every piece hidden**, if the pieces are hidden one by one. The bar
  left out gives the whole replacement its value, and on Android and the
  web its progress bar role
  ([FL-330](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-330), probe fixture p08).
- **The label worked out from the values**, not written as fixed text
  (`bad-stale`).
- **No control inside.** A replacement drops a button's node as well
  (`bad-hides-control`). Where the whole composite is the control, a
  `ListTile` whose subtitle is the replacement is one button named by
  both, on every platform (probe fixture p15).
- **A focus outline** on the add button from its own `style`: Material's
  focus tint on a `TextButton` is under 3:1
  ([FL-430](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-430)).

## Bad examples

Each has one defect, and the tests assert that the defect is present.

| Example | Defect | Fails |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `bad-pieces` | The star row as it is: the stars are not read, and nothing says what 3.4 and 856 are | 1.3.1 and 1.1.1, on every platform |
| `bad-merged` | The bar row in `MergeSemantics`, merged and not replaced: the rating is read with a stray 68 from the bar ([FL-330](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-330)) | none: not a shown failure of a success criterion; shown for what the user hears |
| `bad-not-excluded` | `Semantics(label:)` round the bar row, its pieces not hidden: the rating is read twice, with a stray 68 (FL-330) | none: not a shown failure of a success criterion; shown for what the user hears |
| `bad-hides-control` | A "Reviews" button replaced with the stars: no screen reader can find it, while the keyboard still reaches it | 4.1.2, on every platform |
| `bad-stale` | `good-exclude-semantics` with a fixed label: after a review is added the row shows 857, and a screen reader still hears 856 | 1.3.1, on every platform |

### Shared code

Used by the examples below.

```dart
/// A rating: its average number of stars, out of five, and its number of
/// reviews.
@immutable
class Rating {
  const Rating(this.stars, this.reviews);

  final double stars;
  final int reviews;

  /// The rating as it is shown: "3.4 / 5".
  String get shown => '${stars.toStringAsFixed(1)} / 5';

  /// What the rating means, in words, as the Android references word it:
  /// "Rating: 3.4 out of 5 stars, based on 856 reviews".
  String get words =>
      'Rating: ${stars.toStringAsFixed(1)} out of 5 stars, '
      'based on $reviews ${reviews == 1 ? 'review' : 'reviews'}';

  /// The rating once one more review of [stars] has been added.
  Rating plus(int stars) =>
      Rating((this.stars * reviews + stars) / (reviews + 1), reviews + 1);
}

/// The rating every example starts with.
const startRating = Rating(3.4, 856);

/// The rating as the reference repositories draw it: the word "Rating:", a
/// bar filled to the rating, "3.4 / 5", and "Reviews: 856". The bar is a
/// Material `LinearProgressIndicator`, which has semantics of its own: the
/// value 68, and a progress bar role on Android and the web (none on iOS,
/// FL-256). [piece] wraps each of the four pieces,
/// for `good-hidden-pieces`.
class RatingBar extends StatelessWidget {
  const RatingBar({super.key, required this.rating, this.piece});

  final Rating rating;
  final Widget Function(Widget child)? piece;

  @override
  Widget build(BuildContext context) {
    Widget wrap(Widget child) => piece == null ? child : piece!(child);
    return Wrap(
      spacing: 8,
      runSpacing: 4,
      crossAxisAlignment: WrapCrossAlignment.center,
      children: [
        wrap(const Text('Rating:')),
        wrap(
          SizedBox(
            width: 80,
            child: LinearProgressIndicator(value: rating.stars / 5),
          ),
        ),
        wrap(Text(rating.shown)),
        wrap(Text('Reviews: ${rating.reviews}')),
      ],
    );
  }
}

/// The rating drawn as five stars, then "3.4" and "(856)". An `Icon` with
/// no `semanticLabel` gives assistive technology nothing.
class StarRow extends StatelessWidget {
  const StarRow({super.key, required this.rating});

  final Rating rating;

  @override
  Widget build(BuildContext context) {
    final color = Theme.of(context).colorScheme.primary;
    final full = rating.stars.floor();
    final half = rating.stars - full >= 0.25 && rating.stars - full < 0.75;
    return Wrap(
      spacing: 4,
      crossAxisAlignment: WrapCrossAlignment.center,
      children: [
        for (var i = 0; i < 5; i++)
          Icon(
            i < full
                ? Icons.star
                : (i == full && half)
                ? Icons.star_half
                : Icons.star_border,
            color: color,
          ),
        Text(rating.stars.toStringAsFixed(1)),
        Text('(${rating.reviews})'),
      ],
    );
  }
}

/// A 2 pixel outline in the page's text colour while a `TextButton` has
/// keyboard focus, through the button's own `style`, as in C18: Material's
/// own focus tint on a `TextButton` is under 3:1 against the page
/// (docs/flutter-limitations.md, FL-430).
ButtonStyle focusRing(ColorScheme colors) => ButtonStyle(
  side: WidgetStateProperty.resolveWith(
    (states) => states.contains(WidgetState.focused)
        ? BorderSide(color: colors.onSurface, width: 2)
        : null,
  ),
);

/// Lays out a rating and, under it, a button that adds a 5-star review, so
/// that people and tests can see whether what the rating says follows what
/// it shows. [builder] gets the rating as it is now.
///
/// For the tests alone, an empty node after them whose identifier says how
/// many reviews the row shows, `s10-<slug>-shows-857`: the shown row is
/// hidden from assistive technology, so without it a test could not tell a
/// stale replacement from a button that did nothing. It has no label,
/// value, or action, and is one pixel square (a node of no size is left out
/// of the tree). A new node is made for each count (its key):
/// a change of an identifier alone is not sent to the platform
/// (docs/flutter-limitations.md, FL-331).
class RatingDemo extends StatefulWidget {
  const RatingDemo({super.key, required this.slug, required this.builder});

  final String slug;
  final Widget Function(BuildContext context, Rating rating) builder;

  @override
  State<RatingDemo> createState() => _RatingDemoState();
}

class _RatingDemoState extends State<RatingDemo> {
  var _rating = startRating;

  @override
  Widget build(BuildContext context) {
    final colors = Theme.of(context).colorScheme;
    final shows = 's10-${widget.slug}-shows-${_rating.reviews}';
    return Column(
      crossAxisAlignment: CrossAxisAlignment.start,
      spacing: 8,
      children: [
        widget.builder(context, _rating),
        TestId(
          's10-${widget.slug}-add',
          child: TextButton(
            style: focusRing(colors),
            onPressed: () => setState(() => _rating = _rating.plus(5)),
            child: const Text('Add a 5-star review'),
          ),
        ),
        Semantics(
          key: ValueKey<String>(shows),
          container: true,
          identifier: shows,
          child: const SizedBox.square(dimension: 1),
        ),
      ],
    );
  }
}
```

### `good-exclude-semantics`

```dart
/// The rating row replaced, for assistive technology, by one node that says
/// what it means: `Semantics(excludeSemantics: true)` "replace[s] all child
/// semantics with this node", so the word, the bar (a node of its own, with
/// a value), and the two numbers are hidden, and only the label is exposed. The
/// label is worked out from the same values as the pieces, so it follows
/// them when a review is added.
class GoodExcludeSemantics extends StatelessWidget {
  const GoodExcludeSemantics({super.key});

  @override
  Widget build(BuildContext context) {
    return RatingDemo(
      slug: 'good-exclude-semantics',
      builder: (context, rating) => Semantics(
        identifier: 's10-good-exclude-semantics-rating',
        label: rating.words,
        excludeSemantics: true,
        child: RatingBar(rating: rating),
      ),
    );
  }
}
```

### `good-hidden-pieces`

```dart
/// The same replacement made the other way the Android Compose reference
/// shows: the row is merged into one node (`MergeSemantics`), given the
/// label, and every piece is hidden with `ExcludeSemantics`. Every piece
/// has to be: the bar left out gives the whole node its value, 68, and on
/// Android and the web its progress bar role (docs/flutter-limitations.md,
/// FL-330).
class GoodHiddenPieces extends StatelessWidget {
  const GoodHiddenPieces({super.key});

  @override
  Widget build(BuildContext context) {
    const rating = startRating;
    return MergeSemantics(
      child: Semantics(
        identifier: 's10-good-hidden-pieces-rating',
        label: rating.words,
        child: RatingBar(
          rating: rating,
          piece: (child) => ExcludeSemantics(child: child),
        ),
      ),
    );
  }
}
```

  State<BadHidesControl> createState() => _BadHidesControlState();

class _BadHidesControlState extends State<BadHidesControl> {

    final colors = Theme.of(context).colorScheme;

          label: rating.words,
          excludeSemantics: true,
          child: Wrap(
            spacing: 8,
            crossAxisAlignment: WrapCrossAlignment.center,
            children: [
              const StarRow(rating: rating),
              TextButton(
                style: focusRing(colors),
                onPressed: () => setState(() => _opened++),
                child: const Text('Reviews'),
              ),
            ],
          ),
        ),
        // How many times the button ran, so that people and tests can
        // see that the keyboard reaches it.
        Semantics(
          identifier: 's10-bad-hides-control-opens',
          child: Text('Reviews opened: $_opened'),
        ),
      ],

## What each platform exposes

Measured on Flutter 3.47.5. Screen reader speech is from the probe and
the transcripts below; the rest from the tree tests and the probe
([evidence/probes/s10](../evidence/probes/s10/README.md)).

| | Web | Android | iOS |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A replacement (`good-…`) | one text element holding the words, no role, no tab stop | one `View` named by the words | one static text labelled with the words |
| Screen reader on it | the words alone (VoiceOver, Chromium and WebKit, recorded 2026-11-27) | the words alone | the words alone |
| Stars left as pieces (`bad-pieces`) | two texts, "3.4" and "(856)" | two `View`s | two static texts, read "3.4", "(856)" |
| Merged bar row (`bad-merged`) | one `progressbar` named "Rating: 3.4 / 5 Reviews: 856 68", value 68, read by VoiceOver "Rating: 3.4 / 5 Reviews: 856 68 68% progress indicator" (recorded 2026-11-27) | one `ProgressBar` at 68, read "68.0.", "68,", then the texts | one static text, value "68", read "Rating: 3.4 / 5 Reviews: 856 68" |
| Label with the pieces in (`bad-not-excluded`) | the same, with the words first | the same, with the words after the 68 | the same, with the words first |
| Hidden button (`bad-hides-control`) | no element; after the Tab that reaches it DOM focus stays on Back, and Enter runs Reviews | no node; the focused node stays Back; Tab and Enter run Reviews | no element; two Tabs and Return, and a tap where it is drawn, run it |
| Fixed label (`bad-stale`) | still 856 after the button | still 856 | still 856 |

Things to know:

- **A merged progress indicator brings its value, and its role where the
  platform has one** ([FL-330](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-330)). On iOS
  no progress indicator has a role
  ([FL-256](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-256)), so only the value is
  added there. TalkBack said no role word for the merged node within the
  probe's six seconds.
- **A label and a value** (`Semantics(label: 'Rating', value: …)`) are
  read in a different order on Android, the value first
  ([FL-039](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-039)), and joined into the name
  on the web ([FL-037](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-037)); the references
  give a name only, and so do the examples.
- **Custom actions** on a replacement, which the Compose reference allows
  for a composite's controls, are not on the web
  ([FL-022](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-022)); no example uses one.
- **A chart's or an icon's description** is S02's (its chart, and its
  icon with a time); a custom control's value and adjust actions are
  S14's.

## Flutter limitations

Found on Flutter 3.47.5. Full detail is in the
[limitations log](../flutter-limitations.md).

- [FL-330](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-330): a progress indicator merged into a group puts its value, and its role where the platform has one, on the whole group.
- [FL-331](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-331): a change of a node's identifier alone is not sent to the platform (a test finding, met while building `bad-stale`'s test witness).
- [FL-025](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-025): no counterpart of `accessibilityRepresentation`, a framework control's semantics for a drawing.
- [FL-022](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-022): custom semantics actions are not exposed on the web.
- [FL-256](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-256): no progress indicator has a role on iOS.
- [FL-037](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-037), [FL-039](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-039): a value is part of the name on the web, and read before the label on Android.
- [FL-430](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-430): Material's focus tint on a `TextButton` is faint.
