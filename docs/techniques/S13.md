# S13 Roles and traits

A control says what kind of control it is. The SDK's widgets already do; a
control built by hand gets the role that matches what it does, and the
action that goes with it.

Code: [`lib/techniques/s13_roles_and_traits/examples.dart`](../../lib/techniques/s13_roles_and_traits/examples.dart).
Spec: [`docs/specs/S13-roles-and-traits.md`](../specs/S13-roles-and-traits.md).
In the app: `/t/S13`, and each example at `/t/S13/<slug>`.

A control's name is covered by [S01 Accessible names](S01.md), headings by
[S05 Headings](S05.md), images by [S02](S02.md), [S03](S03.md), and
[S04](S04.md), and everything a button has to do by [C01 Button](C01.md).
Links are left to C03 Stand-alone link.

## Why it matters

A screen reader reads two things for a control: its name and its role.
"Scan barcode, button" tells a person they can press it. "Dark mode, switch,
off" tells them it has two states and which one it is in. The role is also
how people find things: they ask for the next button, and voice control
users say "tap Scan barcode". iOS calls roles, and a few states, "traits".

A `FilledButton`, a `Switch`, or a `Checkbox` has its role built in. A
control drawn by hand, from a `GestureDetector` and some boxes, has none
until `Semantics` gives it one. Three things go wrong:

- It has no role. It works for someone who can see it, and a screen reader
  reads its text as if it were a sentence on the page.
- It has the wrong role. A switch that says it is a button never says
  whether it is on.
- The role and the behaviour do not match. Text that does nothing says
  "button", or a button that works by touch gives assistive technology
  nothing to call. A role is a promise about what the element does.

## WCAG 2.2

- [4.1.2 Name, Role, Value](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#name-role-value): the role of every control can be read by assistive technology, and so can its state. Giving a custom control a role is technique [ARIA4](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/aria/ARIA4); a scripted control with no role is failure [F59](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/failures/F59).

## Good examples

| Example | How | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `good-standard` | `FilledButton`, `SwitchListTile`, and `CheckboxListTile`, with nothing added. Each has its role, its state, and its action. | [iOS: Accessibility Traits](https://github.com/cvs-health/ios-swiftui-accessibility-techniques/blob/main/iOSswiftUIa11yTechniques/Documentation/AccessibilityTraits.md) ("Standard native controls already include accessibility traits by default") |
| `good-cupertino` | `CupertinoButton` and `CupertinoSwitch`. The switch is joined to its text with `MergeSemantics` for its name ([S01](S01.md)); its role is its own. | iOS: Accessibility Traits |
| `good-custom-button` | A control drawn by hand: `Semantics(button: true, enabled: true)` around a `GestureDetector`. The `GestureDetector` gives the tap action that assistive technology calls; `FocusableActionDetector` gives the keyboard. | iOS: Accessibility Traits (the button trait added to an image with a tap gesture), [WCAG ARIA4](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/aria/ARIA4), [MDN: WAI-ARIA roles](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Accessibility/ARIA/Reference/Roles), [Apple: UIAccessibilityTraits](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/uiaccessibilitytraits), [Android: Role](https://developer.android.com/reference/kotlin/androidx/compose/ui/semantics/Role) |
| `good-custom-switch` | A control that is on or off: `Semantics(toggled: value, enabled: true)`. `toggled` is the switch role and its state together. `enabled: true` is needed, or VoiceOver on iOS reads the switch as dimmed. | iOS: Accessibility Traits (the Toggle trait); [APG: Read Me First](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/practices/read-me-first/) (the role matches the behaviour) |
| `good-selected` | Three custom buttons that choose a view: `Semantics(button: true, enabled: true, selected: view == chosen)`. | iOS: Accessibility Traits (the Selected trait) |

Which `Semantics` property gives which role:

| The control | `Semantics` | Web | Android | iOS |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Does something when pressed | `button: true` | `role="button"` | `Button` | Button |
| Is on or off | `toggled: value`, `enabled: true` | `role="switch"` | `Switch` | Switch |
| Is ticked or not | `checked: value`, `enabled: true` | `role="checkbox"` | `CheckBox` | Switch ([FL-035](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-035)) |
| Is the chosen one of several | `selected: value`, with `button: true` | `aria-current` | selected | Selected |
| Goes somewhere | `link: true` and `linkUrl` (left to C03) | `<a href>` | a link only with `linkUrl` | Link |
| Is a heading | `header: true, headingLevel: n` ([S05](S05.md)) | `<h1>` to `<h6>` | heading | Header |
| Is a picture | `image: true` ([S02](S02.md)) | `role="img"` | `ImageView` | Image |

## Bad examples

Each has one defect, and the tests assert that the defect is present.

| Example | Defect | Fails |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `bad-no-role` | The custom control has a tap action and keyboard access and no role. TalkBack and VoiceOver on iOS read "Scan barcode" and do not say "button". The web makes anything with a tap action a button, so the defect cannot be heard there. | 4.1.2 on Android and iOS (F59) |
| `bad-wrong-role` | A switch with `button: true`. It is read as "Dark mode, button", before and after it is switched on. | 4.1.2 |
| `bad-role-on-text` | `Semantics(button: true)` on text that does nothing. It is read as "Free delivery, button", and activating it has no effect. | 4.1.2 |
| `bad-role-on-standard` | `Semantics(button: true)` around a `FilledButton`. The added role is a second button with no name around the real one: an unnamed `Button` on Android, a button inside a button on the web, which VoiceOver reads in Chromium as "Save button, group". VoiceOver on iOS does not stop on it. | 4.1.2 where the unnamed button is exposed |
| `bad-action-hidden` | The button's `GestureDetector` has `excludeFromSemantics: true`. The control reads as a button and works by touch and keyboard, and has no action in the semantics tree. On Android the node has no click action and TalkBack does not offer "Double-tap to activate". On the web a click sent to the element, with no pointer position, does nothing. Whether VoiceOver's own activation command gets through, on the web or on iOS, was not tested. | 4.1.2 |
| `bad-switch-not-enabled` | `Semantics(toggled: value)` with no `enabled`. VoiceOver on iOS reads the working switch as "dimmed". Android and the web are not affected. | 4.1.2 on iOS |

`bad-role-on-text` and `bad-action-hidden` are the two halves of the bad
example in
[iOS: Accessibility Responds To User Interaction](https://github.com/cvs-health/ios-swiftui-accessibility-techniques/blob/main/iOSswiftUIa11yTechniques/Documentation/AccessibilityRespondsToUserInteraction.md):
static text presented as something to operate, and a control taken away
from assistive technology.

/// The state of one example: a count of activations, the switches that are
/// on, and the chosen view.
class _DemoState extends State<_Demo> {
  int _count = 0;
  final Set<String> _on = {};
  String _view = 'Week';

  void press() => setState(() => _count++);

  bool isOn(String name) => _on.contains(name);

  void toggle(String name) => setState(() {
    _count++;
    if (!_on.remove(name)) _on.add(name);
  });

  String get view => _view;

  void choose(String view) => setState(() {
    _count++;
    _view = view;
  });

  @override
  Widget build(BuildContext context) {
    return Column(
      crossAxisAlignment: CrossAxisAlignment.start,
      spacing: 16,
      children: [
        ...widget.builder(context, this),
        Semantics(
          key: ValueKey<String>('s13-${widget.slug}-count'),
          identifier: 's13-${widget.slug}-count',
          liveRegion: true,
          child: Text('Activations: $_count'),
        ),
      ],
    );
  }
}

/// Lays out one example and reports how many times its controls were
/// activated, so people and tests can confirm that activation worked.
class _Demo extends StatefulWidget {
  const _Demo({required this.slug, required this.builder});

  final String slug;
  final List<Widget> Function(BuildContext context, _DemoState demo) builder;

  @override
  State<_Demo> createState() => _DemoState();
}

/// The keyboard half of a custom control: focus, Enter and Space, and a
/// focus ring. It gives the control no role and no tap action; each example
/// adds its own `Semantics` and `GestureDetector` inside it.
class _KeyboardOperable extends StatefulWidget {
  const _KeyboardOperable({required this.onActivate, required this.child});

  final VoidCallback onActivate;
  final Widget child;

  @override
  State<_KeyboardOperable> createState() => _KeyboardOperableState();
}

class _KeyboardOperableState extends State<_KeyboardOperable> {
  bool _focused = false;

  Object? _activate(Intent intent) {
    widget.onActivate();
    return null;
  }

  @override
  Widget build(BuildContext context) {
    final colors = Theme.of(context).colorScheme;
    return FocusableActionDetector(
      mouseCursor: SystemMouseCursors.click,
      // Enter and Space reach the control as these intents through the
      // default shortcuts of WidgetsApp.
      actions: {
        ActivateIntent: CallbackAction<ActivateIntent>(onInvoke: _activate),
        ButtonActivateIntent: CallbackAction<ButtonActivateIntent>(
          onInvoke: _activate,
        ),
      },
      onShowFocusHighlight: (value) => setState(() => _focused = value),
      child: DecoratedBox(
        position: DecorationPosition.foreground,
        decoration: BoxDecoration(
          border: Border.all(
            color: _focused ? colors.onSurface : colors.surface,
            width: 3,
          ),
        ),
        child: Padding(padding: const EdgeInsets.all(5), child: widget.child),
      ),
    );
  }
}

/// What a custom control looks like: an icon and text in an outlined box.
/// The icon has no label, so the text is the control's name.
class _Face extends StatelessWidget {
  const _Face({required this.icon, required this.text, this.strong = false});

  final IconData icon;
  final String text;
  final bool strong;

  @override
  Widget build(BuildContext context) {
    final colors = Theme.of(context).colorScheme;
    return Container(
      constraints: const BoxConstraints(minHeight: 48),
      padding: const EdgeInsets.symmetric(horizontal: 12, vertical: 8),
      decoration: BoxDecoration(
        border: Border.all(color: colors.onSurface, width: strong ? 3 : 1),
      ),
      child: Row(
        mainAxisSize: MainAxisSize.min,
        spacing: 8,
        children: [
          Icon(icon, color: colors.onSurface),
          Flexible(
            child: Text(text, style: TextStyle(color: colors.onSurface)),
          ),
        ],
      ),
    );
  }
}

/// The picture of a switch: its text, and a thumb at the left (off) or the
/// right (on) of a track. The state is shown by position and by an icon, not
/// by colour alone, and has no semantics of its own.
class _SwitchFace extends StatelessWidget {
  const _SwitchFace({required this.text, required this.on});

  final String text;
  final bool on;

  @override
  Widget build(BuildContext context) {
    final colors = Theme.of(context).colorScheme;
    return Container(
      constraints: const BoxConstraints(minHeight: 48),
      padding: const EdgeInsets.symmetric(horizontal: 12, vertical: 8),
      child: Row(
        mainAxisSize: MainAxisSize.min,
        spacing: 12,
        children: [
          Flexible(
            child: Text(text, style: TextStyle(color: colors.onSurface)),
          ),
          Container(
            width: 56,
            height: 30,
            alignment: on ? Alignment.centerRight : Alignment.centerLeft,
            decoration: BoxDecoration(
              border: Border.all(color: colors.onSurface, width: 2),
            ),
            child: Container(
              width: 26,
              height: 26,
              color: colors.onSurface,
              child: Icon(
                on ? Icons.check : Icons.close,
                size: 18,
                color: colors.surface,
              ),
            ),
          ),
        ],
      ),
    );
  }
}

/// The Cupertino controls have their roles as well. The switch has no label
/// of its own, so `MergeSemantics` joins it to the text beside it (S01).
class GoodCupertino extends StatelessWidget {
  const GoodCupertino({super.key});

  @override
  Widget build(BuildContext context) {
    return _Demo(
      slug: 'good-cupertino',
      builder: (context, demo) => [
        TestId(
          's13-good-cupertino-save',
          // Flutter 3.47.5: on web Enter is ButtonActivateIntent, which
          // CupertinoButton does not handle (docs/flutter-limitations.md,
          // FL-002). This adds the missing action; Space already works.
          child: Actions(
            actions: {
              ButtonActivateIntent: CallbackAction<ButtonActivateIntent>(
                onInvoke: (_) {
                  demo.press();
                  return null;
                },
              ),
            },
            child: CupertinoButton.filled(
              onPressed: demo.press,
              child: const Text('Save'),
            ),
          ),
        ),
        TestId(
          's13-good-cupertino-wifi',
          child: MergeSemantics(
            child: GestureDetector(
              behavior: HitTestBehavior.opaque,
              onTap: () => demo.toggle('wifi'),
              child: Row(
                children: [
                  const Expanded(child: Text('Wi-Fi')),
                  CupertinoSwitch(
                    value: demo.isOn('wifi'),
                    onChanged: (_) => demo.toggle('wifi'),
                  ),
                ],
              ),
            ),
          ),
        ),
      ],
    );
  }

## What each platform exposes

Measured on Flutter 3.47.5.

| | Web | Android | iOS |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `FilledButton`, `CupertinoButton`, or a custom control with `button: true` | `role="button"` | `Button` with a click action | button |
| Screen reader on it | "Scan barcode button" | "Scan barcode", "Button", "Double-tap to activate" | "Scan barcode Button" |
| `SwitchListTile`, `CupertinoSwitch`, or a custom control with `toggled` and `enabled: true` | `role="switch"`, `aria-checked` | `Switch`, checkable | switch, value 0 or 1 |
| Screen reader on it | "Dark mode off switch" | "off", "Dark mode", "Switch", "Double-tap to toggle" | "Dark mode Switch button off Double tap to toggle setting." |
| `CheckboxListTile` | `role="checkbox"`, `aria-checked` | `CheckBox`, checkable | switch |
| Screen reader on it | "Remember me unchecked checkbox" | "Remember me", "Check box", "Double-tap to toggle" | "Remember me Switch button off Double tap to toggle setting." |
| Custom button with `selected: true` | `role="button"`, `aria-current="true"` | `Button`, selected | button, selected |
| Screen reader on it | "Week current item button" | "selected", "Week", "Button" | "selected Week Button" |
| Custom control with a tap action and no role | `role="button"` | a clickable `View` | static text |
| Screen reader on it | "Scan barcode button" | "Scan barcode", "Double-tap to activate" | "Scan barcode" |
| `button: true` on text with no action | `role="button"`, no `tabindex`, ignores a click | `Button`, not clickable | button |
| Screen reader on it | "Free delivery button" | "Free delivery", "Button" | "Free delivery Button" |
| `button: true` with the gesture excluded from semantics | `role="button"`, `tabindex="0"`, ignores a click sent to the element | `Button`, not clickable, no click action | button |
| Screen reader on it | "Scan barcode button" | "Scan barcode", "Button" | "Scan barcode Button" |
| `Semantics(button: true)` around a `FilledButton` | a button inside a button, both named "Save" | an unnamed `Button` around the `Button` "Save" | an unnamed button and the button "Save" |
| Screen reader on it | one stop: "Save button, group" in Chromium, "Save button" in WebKit | "Button" on the outer node; "Save", "Button" on the inner | one stop: "Save Button" |
| `toggled` with no `enabled` | `role="switch"`, not disabled | `Switch`, enabled | switch, not enabled |
| Screen reader on it | "Dark mode off switch" | "off", "Dark mode", "Switch" | "Dark mode dimmed Switch button off" |

The web lines are VoiceOver on macOS. TalkBack's lines are what it said with
focus moved to each node by the test.

Things to know:

- **Do not add a role to a widget that has one.** `Semantics(button: true)`
  around a `FilledButton` does not merge with it. It becomes an extra
  button with no name.
- **`toggled` and `checked` need `enabled: true`** on a custom control, or
  VoiceOver on iOS says "dimmed"
  ([FL-033](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-033)). `button: true` does not.
- **The role comes from `Semantics`; the action comes from the gesture.** A
  `GestureDetector` or `InkWell` inside the `Semantics` adds the tap action.
  `excludeFromSemantics: true`, or a raw `Listener`, leaves a button with
  no action for assistive technology to call.
- **The web hides a missing role.** Flutter's web engine gives every node
  with a tap action `role="button"`
  ([FL-006](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-006)). Test the role on Android or
  iOS.
- **A screen reader announces a role whether or not it is true.** Nothing
  on any platform objects to "button" on plain text.
- **On iOS a checkbox is read as a switch**
  ([FL-035](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-035)).
- **`link: true` is not enough for a link** on Android or the web
  ([FL-032](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-032)).

## Flutter limitations

Found on Flutter 3.47.5. Full detail is in the
[limitations log](../flutter-limitations.md).

- [FL-033](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-033): a switch or checkbox with no `enabled` flag is read as dimmed by VoiceOver on iOS. Workaround: `enabled: true`, which `good-custom-switch` uses.
- [FL-035](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-035): a checkbox is a switch for VoiceOver on iOS. No workaround.
- [FL-032](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-032): `Semantics(link: true)` without `linkUrl` is a link on iOS only. Left to C03.
- [FL-034](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-034): `Semantics(keyboardKey: true)` has no effect on any platform, and several other iOS traits have no Flutter counterpart yet. No example.
- [FL-006](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-006): any tappable node is a button on web, so `bad-no-role` cannot be shown there.
- [FL-002](../flutter-limitations.md#fl-002): Enter does not activate a `CupertinoButton` on web. `good-cupertino` adds the missing action for the button. Enter does not toggle a `CupertinoSwitch` there either; Space does.

technology calls (the semantics tap action in the widget tests, a click
sent to the element on the web, `ACTION_CLICK` on Android).
